# SAAGA content workflow deck

Open `index.html` directly in a browser. Keep this folder together: its styles,
controller, logo and Booton fonts are local, with no build step or network dependency.

- Click the slide, press Right/Space, or click Next to advance one point.
- Left/Back reverses one point, including across slide boundaries.
- Click a chapter name to jump to that slide; click a dot to choose a point.
- Press N or click Notes for the current presenter note; Escape closes it.
- Home goes to the beginning; End goes to the recap. Replay restarts explicitly.
- The URL hash retains the slide and point when refreshed.
- Reduced-motion preferences disable animation. Portrait screens use a stacked layout.

The story follows `../../examples/content-job/transcript.txt`, the transcript
fixture used for the original deck. Sample article and search-preview copy are
labeled as illustrative.

Style note retained from the user's direction: **SAAGA Brand Guidelines, page 2**.
The original visual tokens and font files were taken from the sibling
`saaga-landing` app. The logo is the app's actual `public/vector/logo.svg`.
The animation uses fixed header/navigation positions, coordinated point-and-visual
reveals, and short directional slide transitions.
